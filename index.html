6Application and Implementation

6.1 Typical Application: ZvenoAI

zveno.ai

ZvenoAI gives developers and companies in Russia one OpenAI-compatible endpoint for 470+ models, with payment in rubles and invoices with electronic documents. Founder and CTO since November 2025: built the product end to end and runs it.

  • Go service in hexagonal architecture: Fiber, sqlc, PostgreSQL, Kafka.
  • Two-phase billing: a provisional hold when a request arrives, settlement on the actual token count.
  • Payments by card, SBP and invoice; spend analytics per API key and per request.
  • Kubernetes on Selectel, Terraform, ArgoCD; VictoriaMetrics, VictoriaLogs, Grafana, Sentry and Langfuse for observability.
  • Delivery pipeline: a tracker issue becomes an isolated worktree, tests, review and a deploy behind human gates.

6.2 Typical Application: Verdo

verdo.tech

Verdo is an AI platform for law firms. Matters, tasks, mail, documents and firm playbooks live in one shared context that lawyers' own AI agents work with over MCP. CTO through Holocron Lab, a venture lab where Nikita is a partner.

  • Verdo Data: Russian statutes with their editions, case law and agency letters behind an API and an MCP server. It returns the edition in force on a given date where the corpus holds it, checks quotes word for word, and refuses with a reason when the source is missing.
  • Platform pilot in production: mail ingestion, triage into matters, task creation and assignment, text extraction from documents.
  • Court case tracking with a nightly change feed and a durable cursor for downstream automation.
  • Practice agents, for example property-developer disputes from the first claim to the lawsuit, with a lawyer approving each document.
  • TypeScript on Bun, React, PostgreSQL, Python.
